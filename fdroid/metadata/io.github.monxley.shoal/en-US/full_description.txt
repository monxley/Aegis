Shoal is an anonymous, end-to-end-encrypted messenger. There is no phone number,
no email and no account on anyone's server: your identity is a key pair made on
your device the first time you open the app.

The relays that carry your messages cannot read them and cannot tell who they
are between. Every message goes to a fresh one-time address derived from the
recipient's key, so two messages to the same person are unlinkable to anyone but
that person. Traffic is wrapped in fixed-size onion packets, delayed
individually and mixed with cover traffic, so an observer watching the network
still cannot draw a line from you to the person you are talking to.

WHAT THE CRYPTOGRAPHY IS

* ML-KEM-768 (FIPS 203) for post-quantum key encapsulation
* ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204) for post-quantum signatures
* X25519 (RFC 7748) for classical key agreement
* ChaCha20-Poly1305 (RFC 8439) for authenticated encryption
* HKDF-SHA-256 (RFC 5869) for key derivation
* PQXDH handshake, then a Double Ratchet that re-keys from ML-KEM as it runs
* Sphinx onion packets with a LIONESS payload, mixed the Loopix way

Every primitive is implemented from its specification with no third-party
cryptographic libraries, and tested against the published vectors. None of the
constructions are invented here.

FEATURES

* 24-word recovery phrase to back up and restore your identity
* Duress password: a second password opens an empty decoy account
* Panic wipe from the lock screen or Settings
* App disguise: swap the launcher icon and name for a calculator or notes app
* Disappearing messages, synchronised and pruned on both devices
* Safety numbers to rule out a machine-in-the-middle
* Tor and SOCKS5, chainable
* Screenshot blocking, on by default
* Run your own nodes and route exclusively through them
* Biometric unlock, with the key held in the platform keystore
* Voice messages, photos and file attachments

HONEST LIMITS

Shoal is alpha software and has not had an external security audit. The protocol
and its implementation may contain flaws. Anonymity depends on how many people
use the network at the same time, and while that number is small so is the
protection. A truly global, long-running traffic-analysis adversary remains an
open problem in the field: this is strong resistance, not impossibility. And if
someone controls your device while Shoal is unlocked, no protocol helps.

The app checks GitHub Releases for newer versions and offers to open the
download page, because a sideloaded build has no other way to stay current.
